Step 1 · Chunking

How documents are split

Chunking decides what a single search result can contain. Smaller chunks match precise questions; larger chunks give the model more context.

Recursive follows headings, paragraphs and sentences — the safest default. Fixed size cuts every N tokens. Sentence window embeds one sentence but returns its neighbours. Parent–child searches small child chunks and returns the larger parent. Semantic starts a new chunk when the topic shifts.

About 500 tokens ≈ 375 words. Larger chunks carry more context but dilute precise matches.
