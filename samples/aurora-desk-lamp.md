# Aurora Desk Lamp — Product Handbook

The Aurora is a dimmable LED desk lamp with a wireless charging base. This handbook covers
installation, everyday use, troubleshooting and warranty service.

## Box contents

- Aurora lamp head and arm
- Weighted base with Qi charging pad
- 24 V / 2 A power supply with EU and UK plugs
- Quick start card (this handbook is available online)

## Installation

1. Place the base on a flat surface within 1.5 m of a socket.
2. Screw the arm into the base clockwise until it stops. Do not overtighten.
3. Connect the power supply to the barrel jack under the base.
4. Press the touch ring for two seconds. The lamp flashes twice and is ready.

Do not use the lamp with a dimmer switch or an extension lead that also powers a heater.
The power supply is rated for indoor use only.

## Everyday use

Tap the touch ring to toggle the light. Slide clockwise to brighten and anticlockwise to dim.
Press and hold for three seconds to switch between warm and cool colour presets.

Place a phone face-up on the charging pad. A blue ring means charging; an amber ring means
the phone is not aligned or the case is too thick. Charging pauses when the lamp is off.

## Troubleshooting

**The lamp does not turn on.** Check that the barrel jack is fully inserted and the socket
has power. Try a different power supply of the same rating. If the touch ring stays dark,
contact support; do not open the base.

**The wireless charger is slow.** Remove thick cases and metal plates. The pad delivers
15 W only with compatible phones; other phones charge at 5 W or 7.5 W.

**The lamp flickers.** Flicker usually means the power supply is failing or the lamp is on
the same circuit as a large motor. Move it to another socket. If flicker continues, record a
short video and contact support.

**The arm is loose.** Tighten the joint with the supplied 3 mm hex key. Do not force the arm
past its stop; the cable inside can break.

## Cleaning

Wipe with a dry microfibre cloth. Do not use alcohol or abrasive cleaners on the diffuser.
Dust the charging pad with a soft brush; never use water near the base.

## Warranty and repairs

The Aurora carries a two-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. The warranty does not
cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear of the finish. Repairs are handled by
authorised service partners; opening the base voids the warranty.

To start a claim, email support@aurora.example with the order number, a photo of the lamp,
and a description of the fault. We reply within two business days and arrange collection for
warranty repairs.

## Safety

The lamp contains no user-serviceable parts. Unplug before cleaning. Keep the power supply
away from water and do not cover the lamp while it is on. The light source is not replaceable;
when it reaches end of life, return the lamp under our recycling programme.
