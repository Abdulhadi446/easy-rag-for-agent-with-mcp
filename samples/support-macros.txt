SUPPORT MACROS — INTERNAL USE ONLY

Macro 12 — Late delivery
Apologise for the delay. Check the tracking link. If the parcel has not moved for three
business days, open a carrier investigation and offer a replacement or a refund. Do not
promise a delivery date that the carrier has not confirmed.

Macro 14 — Damaged on arrival
Ask for photos of the packaging and the item. If the damage is reported within 48 hours,
send a replacement immediately and arrange collection of the damaged item. Outside 48 hours,
offer a replacement at 50% of list price.

Macro 19 — VAT invoice request
Business customers can download VAT invoices from Billing → Invoices. If the VAT number is
missing, ask them to add it under Company profile and allow one business day for validation.

Macro 23 — Account deletion
Confirm the request in writing. Explain that tax records are kept for seven years and that
the account itself is deleted within 30 days. Escalate to the privacy team if the customer
asks about a data export.

Macro 31 — Escalation to a human
Use this macro when the customer mentions legal action, a regulator, or a security incident.
Do not discuss fault. Collect the order number and contact details, then escalate to the
duty manager. Security incidents go to security@northwind.example immediately.

Macro 40 — Out-of-warranty repair
Quote the standard repair fee: 35 EUR plus parts. Tell the customer the repair has a
90-day guarantee. If the repair costs more than 60% of the current list price, offer a
discount on a new unit instead.
