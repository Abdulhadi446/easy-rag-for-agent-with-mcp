# Northwind Customer Policies

_Last updated: 2026-01-12 · Owner: Customer Operations_

## Refund policy

Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item arrives at our
warehouse. We inspect the item, then send a confirmation email when the refund is issued.
The original shipping fee is not refunded unless the item was faulty or damaged in transit.

Customers outside the EU have 30 days from delivery to request a refund. Inside the EU the
statutory 14-day withdrawal right applies; we extend it to 30 days as a goodwill policy.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Store credit is available on request and is
issued immediately, before the returned item reaches the warehouse.

## Exchanges

Exchanges follow the same window as refunds. If the replacement item costs more, we send a
payment link for the difference. If it costs less, the difference is refunded automatically.
We do not charge a restocking fee for standard catalogue items.

## Invoices and billing

Invoices are issued automatically on the first day of the following month. To change the
billing address on an invoice, open Billing → Invoices → Edit details before the 25th.
Late payments incur a 1.5% monthly interest charge, calculated daily.

VAT is applied based on the delivery address. Business customers can add a VAT number in
Billing → Company profile; validation usually takes one business day.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping arrives the next business
day when ordered before 14:00 CET. We do not ship to PO boxes.

Orders are dispatched from Rotterdam. During peak season (November 20 – December 20),
express orders may take an extra day. Tracking links are sent when the parcel leaves the
warehouse, not when the order is placed.

## Warranty

All hardware carries a two-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Consumables such as
filter pads and batteries are covered for six months. Warranty claims require the order
number and a photo of the fault. Accidental damage is not covered.

## Security and privacy

All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Access to
production records requires hardware-key two-factor authentication and is logged.

We never sell customer data to third parties. Data is retained for seven years for tax
purposes, then deleted. Customers can request an export or deletion by emailing
privacy@northwind.example; we respond within 30 days.
